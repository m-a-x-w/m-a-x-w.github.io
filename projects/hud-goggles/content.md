### Purpose:

The purpose of this project was to create a device to help people with hearing issues be more aware of their enviroment. 

### Technical:



Won honorable mention at TJHSST Science Fair with a working prototype. Had functionality detecting sirens and creating an arrow to demonstrate which direction the sound was coming from.
