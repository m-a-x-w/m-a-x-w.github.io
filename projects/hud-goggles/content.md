### Purpose:

The purpose of this project was to create a device to help people with hearing issues be more aware of their environment. By using machine learning and audio processing, the system helps users identify and locate important sounds in their surroundings, enhancing their spatial awareness and safety.

### Technical:

The project combines several key technologies:

- **Sound Detection**: Utilized TensorFlow Lite for real-time audio classification, optimized for embedded systems to detect various important sounds like sirens, alarms, and vehicle horns.

- **Directional Audio Processing**: Implemented a custom algorithm using multiple microphones to triangulate sound sources, providing accurate directional information with ~15° precision.

- **Visual Interface**: Created a heads-up display (HUD) system using a transparent OLED display mounted on glasses frames, showing directional arrows and sound type indicators in the user's field of view.

- **Embedded System**: Built on a Raspberry Pi 4 with custom 3D-printed case design that seamlessly integrates with the glasses frame. The case houses the main board, battery system, and multiple microphone array while maintaining a comfortable form factor. The system achieves 4+ hours of battery life despite the increased processing power needed for real-time audio analysis.

### Implementation Details:

The custom case design was a crucial component of the project:

- **Ergonomic Integration**: The 3D-printed case was specifically designed to distribute weight evenly across the glasses frame, with the heavier components (battery, Raspberry Pi 4) positioned towards the back for better balance.

- **Thermal Management**: Incorporated ventilation channels in the case design to manage the heat generated by the Raspberry Pi 4 during continuous operation.

- **Modular Design**: The case features a modular design allowing easy access to components for maintenance and upgrades. The microphone array is mounted in detachable pods that can be adjusted for optimal sound detection.

- **Cable Management**: Internal cable routing channels were designed into the case to keep connections secure and maintain a clean aesthetic while protecting the delicate wiring between components.

### Results:

The project won honorable mention at TJHSST Science Fair, demonstrating:
- 94% accuracy in sound classification
- Direction detection within 1-2 seconds
- Successful siren detection with directional indication
- Comfortable wearability for extended periods
